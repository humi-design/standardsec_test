    SEBI Investor Complaint & Dispute Resolution

    File your investment-related complaints quickly and securely through SEBI's official grievance redressal platforms.

    Register on SCORES Login to SMARTODR

    Official SEBI Grievance Redressal Portals

    Learn about SEBI's official platforms for investor grievance redressal and online dispute resolution.

    SCORES Portal

    The SEBI Complaints Redress System (SCORES) enables investors to lodge complaints against listed companies, registered intermediaries, and market participants through an online platform for faster grievance resolution.

    • Easy Online Registration
    • Complaint Tracking
    • Secure Platform
    • Faster Communication
    • Transparent Resolution Process
    Visit SCORES Portal

    SMARTODR Portal

    SMARTODR provides an online dispute resolution mechanism allowing investors and financial entities to resolve disputes efficiently through mediation and arbitration.

    • Digital Dispute Resolution
    • Paperless Process
    • Secure Communication
    • Time Efficient
    • Easy Case Management
    Open SMARTODR

    Information Required Before Filing a Complaint

    Ensure you have the following details ready before submitting your grievance on SEBI portals.

    Full Name

    PAN Number

    Residential Address

    Mobile Number

    Email ID

    Ensure all information is accurate to facilitate quicker grievance resolution.
